The framework

One word. Five phases, two threads, a canvas.

PICASSO reads as one word, but it works on three levels. Five phases you move through in sequence: Pulse, Ignite, Accelerate, Scale and Operate, Orchestrate. Two craft threads that run through every phase, marked below: Change, the human thread, and Sherpa, the delivery thread. And Governance, the canvas beneath all of it. Adoption and embedded delivery were never a single step, so we stopped drawing them as one. Tap any tile for the detail.

Governance is the canvas beneath every phase, and a capability in its own right. It covers ownership, decision rights, policy, controls, risk management, responsible AI, assurance and lifecycle oversight. It runs alongside adoption, not as a supporting topic bolted on later.

It is not a one-off design exercise. Governance evolves as use cases move from experimentation into production and as AI capabilities scale. People move fast precisely because the ground is solid, and mature clients increasingly ask how it works in practice: how ownership sits, how controls evolve, and how capability scales safely over time.

The delivery engine

Change, Accelerate and Sherpa, working as one.

Three motions people used to feel as separate. Defined together, each carries its own outcome and hands cleanly to the next, so cohort capability actually reaches real-world execution.

Change creates the conditions. Accelerate continues the enablement. Sherpa translates capability into execution and client ownership. Three motions, one delivery engine, each with a specific outcome.
The engine room

The AIM Hub is the backplane. It is how PICASSO scales.

Framework direction alone does not close the gap. Capability only becomes repeatable when it is converted into delivery assets, methods and accountabilities field teams can use consistently. The Hub is the backplane and asset engine that does exactly that, and the place the feedback loop compounds.

🛠️

One backplane, feeding every phase.

The Hub codifies what works into reusable assets, then makes them easy for teams to discover, apply and refine. As assets are created, tested and improved through delivery, bespoke reinvention falls away and customer outcomes become consistent and repeatable. Pulse data drives the whole thing: it allocates cohorts and gives the Hub the inputs to generate each phase's delivery agenda, methods and assets.
