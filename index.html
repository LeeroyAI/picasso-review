The people make it work

Craft lives in people, not just models.

PICASSO is people-first by design. Two things carry the whole journey: a baseline every person brings, and a ladder of capability your organisation grows into.

The baseline every person brings

Ask well

Specify the work

State the goal, the audience, the constraints and what good looks like. This is work specification, not prompt decoration.

Plan the work

Design the workflow

Break the job into stages. Decide what AI does, what the human does and what must not be delegated at all.

Check the output

Verify to a standard

Slow down enough to test truth, evidence and fit. Looks right is never a review standard.

Own the decision

Keep the judgement

AI assists. The human owns the risk, the ethics and the outcome. Judgement stays with people.

Usage is not adoption. Better work is.

A dashboard of logins and prompt counts says nothing about whether the work improved. PICASSO measures the things that actually compound.

  • Repeated runs getting measurably better
  • Outputs more grounded in real evidence
  • Review cycles shorter without adding risk
  • Common errors falling, not repeating
  • Skills reused instead of prompts reinvented
  • Agents stopping at the right point
  • Customer promises safer, board claims better evidenced
  • People deciding better with better context

If you cannot measure AI impact, it is just another tool. PICASSO is built to measure it.
