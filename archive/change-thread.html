Usage is not adoption. Behaviour change is.

Logins and prompt counts say nothing about whether the work improved. The Change Thread measures the signals that compound.

  • People choosing AI without being told
  • Peers teaching peers, unprompted
  • New habits holding after the novelty fades
  • Judgement getting sharper with better context
  • Behaviour spreading past the team that started it
  • Champions emerging without being appointed
  • The new way surviving a busy week
  • People owning the decision, not deferring to the tool

If you cannot measure the behaviour shift, you have bought a tool. If you can, you have built a capability.
